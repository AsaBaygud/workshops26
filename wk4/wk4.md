**Data**

The Campaign Against Arms Trade updated its github repository just over a month ago. This is a record of the UK's licences for the export of arms and other 'controlled goods'.

https://github.com/caatdata/uk-arms-export-data

You can simply "Download Zip" from the green "Code" button, for a folder of the eight CSV files to unzip. You don't need to interact with the repository any more than that. Although its README explains the eight files. CAAT also has useful FAQs here: https://caat.org.uk/data/exports-uk/faq.
